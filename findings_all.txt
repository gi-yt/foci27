== LIMITATIONS ==

Thin or missing OONI data: MTNL 0 measurements in its 2025 PW window, You 22, Airtel's PW ASN (AS9498) 4; Airtel results rely on AS24560/AS45609 sharing AS9498's blocklist
Connect's 2025 data comes from 3-5 probes
2026 covers one week only, with no data for You, MTNL, Connect
OONI publishes no probe ID, so probe counts are approximate (loose-strict fingerprint range)
Verdict cutoffs (0.1 / 0.9) are a choice; ISP-resolver results are stable across 0.05-0.3, the 'mostly mixed' disagreement is not (see ROBUSTNESS)
20 of the 723 2025 domain+ISP combos rest on fewer than 3 usable measurements
TLS interference = handshake reset/EOF/timeout after a successful TCP connect; the SNI-filtering signature, but an occasional reset can be a server problem
Resolvers with no ASN (AS0) are counted as internal: 926 of Jio's 5,248 2026 measurements, mostly Jio's own resolvers behind NAT64, so they are missing from the ISP-resolver view
DNS injection, PW false-negative candidates and the Connect resolver explanation are inferences that need confirmation (e.g. by dnsblocks.in re-querying the candidates)
Category associations are weak and their p-values optimistic: the same domain on different ISPs is not independent, and PW's categories are best-effort (see CATEGORIES)
The published PW CSV has 2-12 fewer blocked domains per ISP than the report's Table 4 (same total)
